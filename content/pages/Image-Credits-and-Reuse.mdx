      Global Writing Cultures brings together original project content and material supplied by museums, libraries, archives, scholars, and other sources. The rights that apply depend on what you are reusing.

      GWC TEXT AND DATA

      Except where otherwise noted, original text and data created by Global Writing Cultures are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license: [https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

      You may share and adapt this material for noncommercial purposes if you credit Global Writing Cultures, link to the license, indicate whether you made changes, and distribute adaptations under the same license.

      IMAGES AND OTHER THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

      Images, linked digital surrogates, institutional marks, sponsor logos, trademarks, and other third-party material are not automatically covered by the GWC license. They remain subject to the rights statements of their creators, repositories, holding institutions, and source records. Before reusing an image, follow its source link and confirm the current terms there.

      CITING GLOBAL WRITING CULTURES

      Citations should include the page or record title, Global Writing Cultures, University of Iowa, the stable URL, and the date of access. If a record displays a revision date or data-release version, include it as well.

      MAP DATA — MAPPING PAST SOCIETIES (MAPS)/DARMC

      The MAPS/DARMC Medieval Towns & Routes overlay combines Harvard MAPS/DARMC Major Towns (ca. AD 1200), Early Slavic Mission Routes, Crusade routes (1096–1254), and Viking and Norman movements (7th–10th centuries). MAPS credits Darryl Campbell, Nathaniel Donoghue, Kelly Gibson, Jakub J. Kabala, and Matthew Polk as contributors to these layers. GWC combined the layers, converted them to GeoJSON in WGS84, and generalized the coordinates for web display in 2026. The routes are historical movement reconstructions, not a comprehensive medieval road network.
